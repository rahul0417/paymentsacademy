## How a Card Transaction Works

When you tap your card at a coffee shop, a surprisingly complex chain of events unfolds in milliseconds. The process begins with <GlossaryTerm term="Authorization" definition="A hold placed on funds at the time of a card swipe, dip, or tap." />, where the merchant's terminal sends a request through the card network to your issuing bank.

If approved, the funds are held but not yet transferred. The actual movement of money happens during <GlossaryTerm term="Settlement" definition="The actual transfer of funds between banks, typically the next business day." />, which typically occurs the next business day.

## Key Takeaways

1. **Authorization is not settlement.** The money does not move immediately.
2. **Multiple parties are involved.** Even a simple tap involves at least four entities.
3. **Speed matters.** The entire authorization flow happens in under two seconds.
